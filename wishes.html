Birthday wishes

Everything I'm wishing
for you this year

Joy without reason

May you wake up more mornings than not feeling genuinely happy — not because everything is perfect, but because you are at peace.

Open doors everywhere

Every opportunity you've been reaching for — may this be the year it finally reaches back. The right doors at exactly the right time.

Abundance in all forms

Financial, emotional, spiritual — may this year overflow. You deserve comfort, security, and the luxury of choosing freely.

Love that finds you

Consistent, chosen, kind love. May every relationship add to you, never subtract. You deserve the real thing.

Growth that excites you

May you look back at the end of this year and barely recognise today's version of yourself — in the best possible way.

Protection over your peace

What is meant for you will not miss you. May everything that isn't fall away cleanly, without drama, without loss.
